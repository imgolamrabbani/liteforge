Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026

LiteForge operates on a strict zero-telemetry, 100% local-first architecture.

1. Zero Telemetry & Zero Database Upload

LiteForge does not collect, log, or transmit your SQLite files, schemas, queries, row data, usage analytics, or crash reports. All WebAssembly execution happens locally in memory.

2. Outbound Network Requests

LiteForge makes zero outbound network requests unless you explicitly activate a Polar.sh Pro license key, in which case it communicates only with https://api.polar.sh/v1/customer-portal/license-keys/* to activate, validate (every 72 hours when online), or deactivate your key.
